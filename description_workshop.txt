[h1]Reese[/h1]
Record and replay your Terraria experience

Reese records and saves a replay file while you play and lets you watch that session again from the main menu. 
Reese is built for players and content creators who wish to share their playthroughs, boss fights, or gamemodes with other players.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
Start or host a multiplayer world and play normally. Reese starts recording when a real player joins and stops when the last real player leaves.

[h1]How the recorder works[/h1]
Reese records the server packets that Terraria sends to players. The recorder creates a fake server-side record client with its own socket connection to the server.
The recorder writes server packets to that fake client. That means the replay is pretty much a server-observed replay.

[h1]How the replayer works[/h1]
When you play back a replay, Reese opens that file and feeds the saved packets back into Terraria like a replay server.
This keeps the replay close to normal Terraria behavior while still letting you pause, change speed, step frames, and spectate the world.
Saved replays appear in the Reese replay browser on the main menu. Pick a replay and press Play to watch it.

[h1]Server console commands[/h1]
/record Starts recording
/stoprecording Stops recording
